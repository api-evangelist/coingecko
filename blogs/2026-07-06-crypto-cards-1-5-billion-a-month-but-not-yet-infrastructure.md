Crypto card payments have grown sharply to more than $1.5 billion a month. This report by Tiger Research asks whether that marks crypto cards as genuine financial infrastructure or a model that is still maturing. Key Takeaways Crypto cards resemble debit cards just before their 1990 commercialization, since both use existing payment networks to skip the merchant acceptance bottleneck.

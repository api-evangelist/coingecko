We Are 33 Days In A New Crypto Bull Cycle In our previous study on crypto bear markets , we defined each crypto bear market cycle as any period where Bitcoin trades under its 200 Day Moving Average (200D MA) for 30 or more consecutive days. On August 20, 2026, Bitcoin’s price closed above its 200D MA marking the end of the 2025-2026 crypto bear market cycle at 290 days (November 4, 2025 - August 20, 2026). When we reverse our bear market definition, when Bitcoin trades over its 200D MA for 30 or more consecutive days, is our definition of a crypto bull market.

What Is Safe? Safe (formerly Gnosis Safe) is a smart contract wallet that requires a minimum number of designated signers to approve a transaction before it executes (an "M-of-N" setup). Safe has become one of the most widely used pieces of infrastructure in crypto, with well over a trillion dollars in transaction volume processed and tens of millions of Safe accounts deployed across chains.

Real World Assets (RWA) are blockchain tokens representing ownership of tangible physical assets or traditional financial instruments such as real estate, government bonds, or gold, enabling 24/7 trading, fractional ownership, and instant DeFi settlement. As of Q1 2026, the combined RWA market exceeds $320 billion, with tokenized RWAs (excluding stablecoins) growing 256.7% over 15 months from $5.42 billion in January 2025 to $19.32 billion by March 2026, driven largely by tokenized treasuries like BlackRock's BUIDL.

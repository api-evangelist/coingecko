It builds optimization strategies and platforms for protocols, applications, and chains, while its economic risk models protect billions in decentralized finance. The same research and risk expertise now powers Gauntlet's onchain vaults, both those curated on protocols such as Morpho and those running on Aera, Gauntlet's first-party vault infrastructure, which deliver risk-adjusted yield to vault suppliers.

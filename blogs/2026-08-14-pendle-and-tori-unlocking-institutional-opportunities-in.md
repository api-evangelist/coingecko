Overview of Pendle x Tori Finance Tori Finance brings institutional trading-desk returns on-chain, and Pendle lets you trade that yield as fixed or floating. Tori runs delta-neutral strategies: money-market carry, futures arbitrage, and calendar spreads, the same playbook used by desks managing pension and sovereign capital, and channels the returns into an on-chain synthetic dollar, trUSD. Staking trUSD mints strUSD: a token that automatically compounds that strategy yield.

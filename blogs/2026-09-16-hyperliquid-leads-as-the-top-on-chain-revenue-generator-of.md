The top 15 revenue-generating crypto projects captured 56.02% of the $3.40 billion total tracked across all non-stablecoin-issuer projects between January 1 and September 15, 2026. Hyperliquid leads by a wide margin, generating $429.04 million YTD, 12.62% of the entire pool and more than the next two highest earners combined. Hyperliquid and Pump.fun together account for $751.25 million, or 22.10% of all 2026 revenue tracked.

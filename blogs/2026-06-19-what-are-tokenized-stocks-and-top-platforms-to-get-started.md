What Are Tokenized Stocks? Tokenized stocks are digital representations of traditional company shares on blockchain networks – think of them as crypto versions of Tesla or Apple shares. Fastest-growing coin category on CoinGecko: Tokenized stocks surged +3,314% from just 14 to 478 listed coins between January 2024 and May 2026, outpacing even AI and RWA categories.

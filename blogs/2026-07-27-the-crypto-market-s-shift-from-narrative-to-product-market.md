In the first half of 2026, no single narrative ran through the entire market. Even so, stablecoins, RWA, meme tokens, DeFi, and prediction markets each survived or grew despite the downturn. This report by Tiger Research looks at the 2026 crypto market as one that is no longer driven by a single narrative but is instead searching for product-market fit across a diversified set of sectors.

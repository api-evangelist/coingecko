Pumpfun’s market leader status is being challenged once more, with new entrants Ponsfamily and Stonkfun overtaking Pumpfun in daily launchpad revenue. Pons, which was launched only two months ago on July 14, 2026 overtook Pumpfun in daily protocol revenue on September 2, 2026 with $1.1M in revenue. More impressively, Stonkfun, which was only launched last month on August 3, overtook Pumpfun in daily protocol revenue on September 6, 2026 with $1.5M in revenue.

Nansen is an agentic trading platform that brings onchain analytics and execution, allowing investors to trade everything onchain with AI. Powered by 500 million labeled addresses with real-time data across major networks, Nansen helps investors, funds, and protocols turn complex onchain activity into actionable insights, providing visibility into market movements, wallet activity, and capital flows - right alongside trading. Its AI-powered features enable users to discover opportunities, execute trades, and manage portfolios through a single platform more efficiently.

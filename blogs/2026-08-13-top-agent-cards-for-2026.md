What Are Agent Cards? Agent cards are scoped payment credentials that let an AI agent spend on a consumer's behalf without ever holding the consumer's real card number. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Stripe all launched their own agent card programs between 2025 and 2026, each with different rules on reusability, approval flow, and fees.

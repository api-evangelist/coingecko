What is the Bitcoin Rainbow Chart? The Bitcoin Rainbow Chart is a long-term valuation model that uses rainbow-colored, logarithmic bands to visualize Bitcoin's price and indicate potential trading opportunities. Origin: The Bitcoin Rainbow Chart is a Bitcoin price valuation tool made in 2014 by Bitcointalk user "trolololo" and Reddit user "azop".

Overview of Pendle x Plume Plume brings institutional real-world assets on-chain, and Pendle lets you trade the yield they generate. Plume tokenizes real-world assets like private credit and trade receivables, making institutional yield accessible on-chain through its Nest vaults. Pendle splits any yield-bearing asset into two tokens: PT for fixed returns and YT for floating yield exposure.

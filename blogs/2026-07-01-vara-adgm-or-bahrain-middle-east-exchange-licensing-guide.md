The Middle East has become one of the most closely watched regions for crypto exchange expansion. Dubai’s VARA (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority) licensing regime, in particular, has attracted strong industry attention as major exchanges look for regulated footholds in the UAE. For many operators, it is now one of the first jurisdictions that comes up in any serious licensing roadmap discussion.

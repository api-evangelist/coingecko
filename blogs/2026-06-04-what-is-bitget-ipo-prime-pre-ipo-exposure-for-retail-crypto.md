Bitget IPO Prime is a subscription platform providing tokenized economic exposure to select private companies before their IPO, launching tokens on the Solana blockchain through Republic, a regulated tokenization platform. Each token represents contractual economic rights backed 1:1 by underlying assets, offering retail users price performance exposure without conferring shareholder status, dividends, or voting rights in the underlying companies.
